Octop is an open-source AI assistant you host yourself. It gives you a web dashboard, a CLI, and IM integrations (Feishu, DingTalk, QQ, Discord, WeCom) — all running in a single process, with every conversation and credential stored on your own machine under `~/.octop/`.

## What you can do with Octop

- **Chat with specialized agents** — create multiple agents per user, each with its own LLM provider, skill set, and MBTI persona
- **Connect your messaging apps** — route conversations from Feishu, DingTalk, QQ, Discord, or WeCom into your agents
- **Schedule automated tasks** — set cron jobs that prompt your agents on a schedule
- **Integrate with your IDE** — use ACP to let Zed, OpenCode, or Claude Code talk to your Octop agents
- **Automate the web** — headless browser sessions and remote desktop support built in
- **Stay private** — no cloud dependency; all data lives on your machine

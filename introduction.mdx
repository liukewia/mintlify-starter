Octop is an open-source AI assistant you host yourself. It gives you a web UI, a CLI, and messaging integrations (Feishu, WeCom, DingTalk, QQ, WeChat, Telegram, and more) — all running in a single process, with every conversation and credential stored on your own machine under `~/.octop/`.

## What you can do with Octop

- **Chat with specialized agents** — create multiple agents per user, each with its own LLM provider, skill set, and MBTI persona
- **Connect your messaging apps** — route conversations from Feishu, WeCom, DingTalk, QQ, WeChat, Telegram, and more into your agents
- **Schedule automated tasks** — set cron jobs that prompt your agents on a schedule
- **Delegate to coding agents** — use ACP to connect OpenCode, CodeBuddy, Claude Code, or Codex
- **Automate the web** — a built-in browser workbench, terminal, and remote desktop
- **Stay private** — no cloud dependency; all data lives on your machine
